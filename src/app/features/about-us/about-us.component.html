About Us

AA Gwarzo 5-Aside Arena is a premier mini-football facility located in Aliko Dangote University of Science, Wudil. Our mission is to promote fitness, foster community spirit, and offer a top-notch environment for football lovers of all levels — from casual players to seasoned competitors.

We’re built for fun, fitness, and football.

At AA Gwarzo 5-Aside Arena, we believe in creating an exciting, inclusive environment where people can come together to play, connect, and thrive. Whether you're organizing a friendly match, participating in a local tournament, or hosting a private event — our facilities are equipped to deliver an unforgettable experience.

We are committed to maintaining a safe and modern football pitch, investing in customer experience, and making sports more accessible to our growing community in Gwarzo and beyond.
