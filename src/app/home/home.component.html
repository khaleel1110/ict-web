You can report an ICT problem by logging into the support system, selecting the appropriate complaint category, providing details about the issue, and submitting the complaint. You can also provide additional information that can help the ICT team understand and resolve the problem.
After submitting a complaint, you can track its progress through your dashboard. The system displays the current status of the complaint, allowing you to know whether it is pending, assigned, in progress, or resolved.
Submitted complaints are reviewed by authorized ICT staff. Based on the nature of the problem, a technician can be assigned to handle the issue. The technician works on the complaint and updates its status until the problem is resolved.
You can report different types of ICT-related problems, including hardware faults, software issues, network problems, system access issues, and other technical problems affecting your work or learning activities.
The complaint status is updated by the assigned ICT technician or authorized staff. Once the issue has been resolved, the complaint will be marked as resolved and the user can view the updated status from their dashboard.
